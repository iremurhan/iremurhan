# Beyza İrem Urhan

Researcher working on **vision–language models**: how image and text representations are aligned, where they fail, and how lightweight adaptation changes them. MSc in Computer Engineering, Boğaziçi University (2026).

## Research interests

- Vision–language representation learning (CLIP-style contrastive models, JEPA-style predictive objectives)
- Cross-modal retrieval and its diagnostics: compositionality, missing positives, alignment and uniformity
- Regularisation for joint embedding spaces (VICReg, SIGReg)
- Computer vision, and careful evaluation of what a method actually improves

## Selected work

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [clip-retrieval-lab](https://github.com/iremurhan/clip-retrieval-lab) | MSc thesis code: *Cross-Modal Retrieval: Interventions, Diagnostics, and Representational Limits* (2026). CLIP ViT-B/16 and ViT-L/14 fine-tuning on COCO and Flickr30k, with caption rewrites, hard negatives, multi-label and segment-aware variants, evaluated on SugarCrepe, MMVP-VLM and cross-dataset transfer. |
| [clip-product-image-text-matching](https://github.com/iremurhan/clip-product-image-text-matching) | Fine-tuning CLIP to detect mismatched product images and titles, comparing cross-entropy and contrastive objectives on an image-disjoint split. |
| [svhn-handcrafted-features](https://github.com/iremurhan/svhn-handcrafted-features) | Hand-crafted image descriptors (HOG, LBP, GLCM, Gabor) vs. classical classifiers on SVHN digits. |
| [pan19-authorship-attribution](https://github.com/iremurhan/pan19-authorship-attribution) | Cross-domain authorship attribution on PAN-19 with character n-grams, SVM ensembles and a reject option. |
| [ngram-language-identification](https://github.com/iremurhan/ngram-language-identification) | From-scratch Cavnar–Trenkle language identification for nine languages, with a snippet-length evaluation. |
| [cancer-biomarker-feature-selection](https://github.com/iremurhan/cancer-biomarker-feature-selection) | Cross-validated, stability-based gene ranking on microarray data (BSc senior project). |
